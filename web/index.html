Drone Locator

Search a map of the area for a lost aircraft

1. Add your search map

Pick all the photos from your search flight (straight-down photos with GPS, 5 to 200). Our server stitches them into one map, usually in 5 to 20 minutes. Your photos are deleted from our server as soon as the map is made. You can keep 4 maps for 12 hours; a new one replaces your oldest map.

  1. Upload photos
  2. Match photos
  3. Stitch the map
  4. Load the map

Already have one big stitched picture of the area? Tap I already have a map. It is uploaded once and reused for later searches. You can keep 4 maps; a new one replaces your oldest map.

More options

If the file has no location built in, you can type the four corners of the area so we can give walking directions later. Enter west, south, east, and north, in that order.

2. Show us your drone

On a phone, tap Draw box, then drag on the map. On a computer, tap Draw box or hold Shift and drag. Or upload a close-up photo of your drone or a marking on it.

3. Find it

Search turns on after step 1 (load a search map) and step 2 (draw a box or upload a photo of your drone).

The search runs on the server, so a large map will not freeze your phone. Turn on “Look for this color” to hunt for your drone’s paint against the field.

More options

Use these only if you know the size of a small marking, how detailed the map is, or how high the photos were taken.

Matches

Top matches will appear here with a score, a box on the map, and a location you can walk to when the map has GPS.
